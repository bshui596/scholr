# SCHOLR CODE OF CONDUCT
## Our Standard

Scholr is intended to be a welcoming and respectful project for students, developers, contributors, and users.

## Everyone participating in the project is expected to:

Be respectful and considerate.
Welcome people with different experience levels.
Give constructive feedback.
Avoid harassment, discrimination, insults, or personal attacks.
Respect other people's privacy.
Keep discussions focused on improving Scholr.
Unacceptable Behaviour

## The following are not tolerated:

Harassment or bullying.
Discriminatory or hateful comments.
Threats or intimidation.
Personal attacks.
Sharing private information without permission.
Deliberately disrupting the project or community.
Reporting

### If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the project maintainers through the project's available communication channels.

### Maintainers may remove comments, restrict participation, or take other appropriate action when this Code of Conduct is violated.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to the Scholr project, its repositories, discussions, contributions, and related community spaces.

By participating in Scholr, you agree to follow these standards.
